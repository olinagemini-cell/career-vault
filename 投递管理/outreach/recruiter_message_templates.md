# Recruiter Outreach — LinkedIn Message 模板

> 用途：搭配 `recruiter_channel_list.md` 使用
> 原则：简短、自我定位清晰、不提薪资、不显急迫

---

## 一、Connect Invite（300 字符上限）

LinkedIn connect invite 有字符限制（300 chars），所以要极致精炼。下面是 4 个版本，按 recruiter 类型选用。

### 🅐 默认版（Robert Half / Hays / Michael Page / Morgan McKinley 通用）

```
Hi {{First Name}}, I'm a Program / Delivery Manager with 14 years 
in IT delivery — financial services (fintech + leasing) and MNC 
cross-border programs. Currently exploring senior delivery / 
program roles in Shanghai. Would value staying connected for any 
relevant opportunities. Thanks!
```

字符数：≈285。

### 🅑 金融纵深突出版（Morgan McKinley / Hays 的 banking 专线）

```
Hi {{First Name}}, I'm a Senior PM with 10 years in financial 
services IT (6 yrs fintech + 4 yrs equipment-leasing) — currently 
at BPCE running cross-border delivery from Shanghai. Open to 
senior delivery / BRM roles in banking, insurance or consulting. 
Happy to connect.
```

字符数：≈290。

### 🅒 乙方 consulting 突出版（Robert Half / Michael Page 的 technology consulting）

```
Hi {{First Name}}, I'm a Program Manager with 14 years in IT 
delivery — 6 yrs vendor-side at NCR managing $50M+ portfolios, 
now in-house at BPCE. Strong dual perspective on client and 
vendor delivery. Exploring senior Delivery / Engagement Manager 
roles in Shanghai.
```

字符数：≈295。

### 🅓 已合作 recruiter 跟进版（Robert Half 已对接过的）

```
Hi {{First Name}}, thanks again for the Delivery Manager role 
we discussed last week. I'm broadening my search — open to other 
senior delivery / program roles in Shanghai (financial services, 
consulting, MNC IT). Let me know if anything else fits.
```

字符数：≈275。

## 二、Accept 后第一封 message（Connect 通过后发）

> 时机：对方 accept 之后 1-2 天内发，不要立即发（会显得脚本化）
> 内容：用 1 段 + 简历附件

### 模板

```
Hi {{First Name}}, thanks for connecting.

Quick context — I'm a Program / Delivery Manager based in Shanghai 
with 14 years of IT delivery experience:

• 6 yrs vendor-side at NCR running a $50M+ portfolio across two 
  major KA accounts + several mid-sized clients in Greater China; 
  3 consecutive years of #1 APAC portfolio ROI
• Currently at BPCE Equipment Solutions (French banking group), 
  leading the China end-to-end rollout of a global core system 
  under HQ cost-shared governance, reporting to the COO
• Bilingual CN/EN, strong on RFP / QBR / SLA governance and 
  multi-country stakeholder coordination

I'm exploring senior Delivery / Program / Engagement Manager 
roles — open to consulting (Accenture / Capgemini / TCS / Infosys 
type firms), MNC in-house IT, or financial-services tech.

I've attached my one-page CV for reference. Happy to chat if 
anything in your pipeline fits — or to stay on your radar for 
future roles.

Best,
Olina
```

字数：约 150 words。附件挂 `Olina_Liu_Shanghai_Vendor_IT_Delivery_EN.docx`。

## 三、Follow-up（14 天后没回的）

> 用途：对 P0 recruiter 一次 follow-up；P1 不主动 follow

### 模板

```
Hi {{First Name}}, circling back on this — wanted to see if 
anything in your current pipeline matches the senior Delivery / 
Program Manager profile I shared. 

For context, I'm not in a rush, but staying actively informed of 
the market in Shanghai (consulting & MNC IT delivery). Happy to 
have a brief intro call any time it's convenient.

Best,
Olina
```

字数：约 65 words。**只发一次**，没回就放着，3 个月后再启动。

## 四、定制化字段速查

| 占位符 | 怎么填 |
|--------|--------|
| `{{First Name}}` | LinkedIn profile 上的 first name |
| `the {{role}} role we discussed` | 如果有过沟通历史，引用具体岗位名 |

---

## 五、Outreach 反例（不要这样写）

❌ "I noticed your profile and was very impressed..." — AI/模板感太强，被秒删
❌ "I have 14 years of experience in project management..." — 没有差异化，所有 PM 都这么说
❌ "I am open to opportunities and would love to chat" — 没给对方任何理由"投资 30 分钟跟你聊"
❌ "I expect salary around X" — 第一封信不要谈薪
❌ 群发版本不改 first name —— 会被立刻识破
